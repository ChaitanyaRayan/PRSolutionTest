G-BA "Not Proven" — Tevimbra in First-Line ES-SCLC (Germany)

Germany's Federal Joint Committee rated Tevimbra's added benefit as "Not Proven" for first-line ES-SCLC because RATIONALE 312 used chemotherapy alone rather than the AMNOG-defined comparator. Sustained clinical data coverage appeared across NSCLC, ESCC, HCC, and nasopharyngeal cancer — reinforcing the molecule's multi-indication breadth.

The competitive landscape was materially shaped by Akeso's HARMONi-6 data confirming ivonescimab's superiority over tislelizumab in first-line squamous NSCLC.
